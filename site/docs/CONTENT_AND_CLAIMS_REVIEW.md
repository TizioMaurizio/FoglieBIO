# Content and claims review

Review date: 2 October 2026. Evidence classification for this implementation; not a certification of legal compliance or independent laboratory verification.

## Verified from supplied materials

The original `IL GRANDE DONO_LARUOTABIO.docx` was extracted from OOXML. Its founder section (extracted paragraphs around lines 117–124 in the local audit) states: Antonio Berti previously worked as a mechanical designer, chose organic agriculture in 1998, began studying olive leaves in 2013, and created Foglie Bio Plus in 2022 after experimentation and consultation. These facts appear in the timeline and narrative. No founder quotes, qualifications or invented incidents were added.

The DOCX describes oleuropein, hydroxytyrosol, flavonoids and phenolic compounds. The landing treats them as background about the plant, with no product concentration, effective dose or clinical outcome. The document's references were not independently validated; it does not establish testing of the finished product.

[Official product page](https://www.laruotabio.it/foglie-bio-plus/) was retrieved directly after the web reader failed. It supplies the bottle and label images. These are public source facts, not independently audited production records.

The old [experimental landing](https://tiziomaurizio.github.io/FoglieBIO/) was retrieved. Its `assets/team.jpg` returns 404. Its stronger health, dose-equivalence and pesticide statements were not reused. The workspace held the DOCX and brief, with no separately attached founder/context photographs or full TerraElix specification. Visual direction therefore follows the TerraElix behaviors described in the brief.

## External review sources

The [EU health claims register](https://food.ec.europa.eu/food-safety/labelling-and-nutrition/nutrition-and-health-claims/eu-register-health-claims_en) describes authorized/non-authorized claims and conditions. The [Italian Ministry claims guidance](https://www.salute.gov.it/new/it/tema/nutrizione/indicazioni-nutrizionali-e-sulla-salute-claims/) explains the framework for labels and advertising. The [botanicals guidance](https://www.salute.gov.it/new/it/tema/alimenti-fini-medici-speciali-ed-integratori/sostanze-e-preparati-vegetali/) addresses permitted plants/preparations. The [notification FAQ](https://www.salute.gov.it/new/it/faq/faq-alimenti-soggetti-notifica-attraverso-nsis-nuovo-sistema-informativo-sanitario/) clarifies that notification is not product approval. No claim-by-claim authorization for this formulation was established, so no health benefit claim was adopted.

[Google Ads policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15595718?hl=it) restricts misleading or unapproved supplement promotion; a scholarly citation is not an ad approval. The [Meta health/wellness policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/health-wellness/) could not be fetched (429). Its current wording must be checked before campaigns; this implementation does not claim a completed Meta eligibility assessment. No ads were launched.
