No claim-by-claim authorization for this formulation was established, so no health benefit claim was adopted.

[Google Ads policy](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/15595718?hl=it) restricts misleading or unapproved supplement promotion; a scholarly citation is not an ad approval. Its current wording must be checked before campaigns; this implementation does not claim a completed Meta eligibility assessment. No ads were launched.
