# Strategy and research

Research snapshot: 2 October 2026. The category review informed architecture, not product facts or legal claims. Prices below were observed on the cited pages and can change. No competitor checkout was submitted.

## Existing experience

The official website is a broad catalogue with long health-oriented copy. The prior experimental landing has clearer product focus but repeats unsupported health promises, dose equivalences and broken team imagery. The new landing prioritizes a real product, Italian leaf origin and the documented founder journey. A clean cream/olive editorial system replaces dense health claims and generic trust badges.

The strongest distinction available in our evidence is Antonio's personal transition into agriculture and long curiosity about olive leaves. Repeated category motifs such as sweeping antioxidant promises, customer testimonials and clinical language are not substitutes for product evidence. The review covered public catalogue/story surfaces; subscriptions, checkout speed, retention and ad performance were not measured. No claim of a comprehensive market survey is made.

## Information architecture and visual system

Hero → three perspectives → why leaves → supported origin/process → Antonio → botanical context → purchase and usage details → FAQ → final purchase invitation → newsletter/footer. Cream #F4F3EB, olive #293E2E, muted leaf greens; DM Sans headlines and Inter body/UI. Large hero lettering, circular linework and real bottle, warm editorial panels. Native details and dialog components keep controls understandable. Motion uses CSS and a three-state carousel with pause/manual controls and reduced-motion support.

## Ads and video roadmap

Google intent: branded product and olive-leaf category pages first. Keep educational search intent separate from unsupported disease-oriented promises. Meta creative should start with Antonio, the actual leaf, and the product. No diagnosis-based personalized copy or fabricated testimonials. Future ads need current platform and legal review, approved commercial terms and working live checkout before launch.

- 15 seconds: real leaf closeup → Antonio at work → bottle. On-screen: “Una storia coltivata nel tempo.” / “Foglie Bio Plus®”.
- 30 seconds: Antonio describes his 1998 career change in his own approved words; leaf detail and bottle; link to the story.
- 60 seconds: 1998 choice, 2013 curiosity, 2022 product; verified real collection and lab footage; product details and a calm invitation to learn more.

These are storyboards, not factual interview transcripts. Record Antonio rather than using an actor; obtain his approval for any spoken line. Do not depict an undocumented manufacturing step. No ads, tracking, emails or campaigns were created.
